# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Fugal welcomes constructive participation regardless of background or experience. Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss technical decisions with evidence, and make room for questions and disagreement.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or using project spaces for spam or unrelated promotion.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the Fugal project.

## Enforcement

For ordinary moderation concerns, contact the maintainers through the repository. For sensitive reports, use [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/fugal-ai/fugal-subnet/security/advisories/new) and state that the report concerns conduct rather than a software vulnerability.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the project or its community. Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as practical.
